Exhibition Stand Project Manager

Gulf Stand Builders

  • LocationDubai, United Arab Emirates
  • CategoryExhibition & Stands
  • TypeFull-time
  • SalaryAED 15,000–20,000 / month

Manage the design, build and on-site installation of bespoke exhibition stands for international trade shows.

Key responsibilities

  • Coordinate stand design, fabrication timelines and on-site build & breakdown
  • Liaise with clients, designers and the workshop to hit show deadlines
  • Manage installation crews and enforce site safety standards
  • Track project costs and deliver stands on time and on budget

What you’ll need

  • 4+ years in exhibition stand build or fit-out project management
  • Ability to read technical drawings and CAD layouts
  • Experience working to fixed trade-show deadlines
  • UAE driving licence preferred
