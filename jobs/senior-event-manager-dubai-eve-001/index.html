Lead end-to-end delivery of large-scale conferences and exhibitions across the UAE and wider Gulf region.

Key responsibilities

  • Own the full event lifecycle for exhibitions and conferences from brief to post-event reporting
  • Manage budgets of AED 1M+ and negotiate with venues, contractors and suppliers
  • Lead cross-functional teams of coordinators, production crew and on-site staff

What you’ll need

  • 6+ years managing large exhibitions or conferences, ideally in the GCC
  • Proven budget ownership and supplier negotiation experience
  • Strong knowledge of Dubai World Trade Centre and ADNEC operations
  • Fluent English; Arabic is an advantage
